# School Secretary

School Secretary is a desktop study planner for students at any school that uses Brightspace. It reads your own courses and keeps what's due, your exams, your classes and your instructors' posts in one place on your computer.

**Unofficial.** School Secretary is not made by D2L (the company behind Brightspace) and is not made, endorsed or approved by any school. It never completes assignments, labs or quiz answers. It explains the task and points you to your course materials. You still do the work.

## Free and Pro

School Secretary is free to download and use.

- **Free:** deadlines, exams, classes and instructor posts kept on your computer; study lock-in, timers and a morning list; a calendar file you can add to Google Calendar.
- **Pro (coming later, not available yet):** a study plan, "Right now", quick updates every few hours, Chat that answers from your syllabi and files, and a weekly review.

## Download

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/gandolfifg-ops/school-secretary/releases/latest).
2. Download the zip for your computer: `SchoolSecretary-windows.zip`, `SchoolSecretary-mac.zip` or `SchoolSecretary-linux.zip`.
3. Unzip it and open School Secretary.
4. Enter your school's Brightspace address, then sign in through your school's normal login page.

## Opening it the first time

The downloads are not code-signed yet, so your computer shows a warning the first time.

- **Windows:** if "Windows protected your PC" appears, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** right-click (or Control-click) School Secretary and choose **Open**, then **Open** again. Or try to open it once, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

## Updates

School Secretary checks this page for new versions and updates itself. You can also download the newest zip from [Releases](https://github.com/gandolfifg-ops/school-secretary/releases) at any time.

## Privacy

School Secretary runs on your own computer. You sign in to Brightspace yourself, in a normal browser window, and your course data stays on your computer. Settings has **Wipe school data** to remove the saved sign-in, the database and cached files. AI features are optional and use your own AI account key.

The person in charge of protecting personal information for School Secretary is its owner. Write to schoolsecretary.official@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. The full Privacy page is inside the app, under Settings.

School Secretary is for people 16 and older.

Some schools limit automated access or third-party tools. Check your school's acceptable-use and academic-integrity policies.
